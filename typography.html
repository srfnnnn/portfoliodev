Sketch of the Typography


The inspiration for this design came from my strong appreciation for flowers, their inherent beauty, diversity, and the way their colours effortlessly enhance any environment. I've always been enchanted by flowers, not only for their beauty but also for the feelings they inspire - serenity, happiness and creativity. To convey this personal bond, I decided to integrate my name into this artwork, using the letters of my name as the foundation. Each letter is artistically crafted using various flowers and leaves, transforming something as simple as a name into a piece of floral art. This combination allowed me to merge something personal with something I truly love, leading to a design that showcases both my individuality and my passion for the elegance and charm of nature.


Typography 1

It showcases a lively combination of purples, greens and delicate teal highlights. The shades of purple and lavender evoke feelings of serenity, creativity and luxury. The use of shades of green in the leaves creates a strong natural contrast symbolizing freshness and balance. Teal highlights further enhance the design by adding a hint of brightness and modernity. These colours create a lively and harmonious floral arrangement that feels both refreshing and engaging.

Colour scheme: Complementary

Typography 2

It features a harmonious palette of shades of purples. This colour is frequently linked to creativity, elegance and imagination. As is acts as the primary colour, it imparts a polished and artistic quality to the piece. This colour palatte expresses an air of elegance and charm, making it ideal for themes that aim to blend floral beauty with a modern and refined vibe.

Colour scheme: Monochromatic

Typography 3

It uses a nature-themed colour scheme led by rich reds, earthy greens. The deep reds evoke feelings of passion, elegance and warmth while the green leaves symbolize development, energy and balance. Teal highlights further enhance the design by adding a hint of brightness and modernity. These colours create a lively and harmonious floral arrangement that feels both refreshing and engaging.

Colour scheme: Analogous
